# SwiftCart for Customers

*A brief for shoppers.*

---

## The offer

**One app. Every shop near you. Delivered.**

Order from restaurants, pharmacies, supermarkets, minimarts and more — wherever you are in
the country. Pay in the app, watch your rider on the map, get it delivered.

---

## What you get

### Everything near you, in one place
Open the app and see every store around you, sorted by distance. Change your location from
the picker at the top and you'll see the shops near *there* instead — useful when ordering
to somewhere other than home.

### Real search and real browsing
Search by shop, product or category. Filter by what's open now. Save your favourite stores.

### Pay how you want
Card, bank transfer, USSD, or your SwiftCart wallet balance. Payment is handled securely
by Paystack — SwiftCart never stores your card.

### Watch it happen
Live map tracking from the moment a rider accepts: to the shop, collected, on the way to
you. You get a delivery code to confirm handover, so nobody else can collect your order.

### Get rewarded
Earn loyalty points on every order, climb tiers, and redeem rewards. Refer friends. Get
discounts, bundles and flash sales from shops you like.

### If something goes wrong
Open a dispute in the app. Refunds are credited to your SwiftCart wallet, usable on your
next order. There's in-app support and an order history with every receipt.

---

## What it costs

You pay for **your items** plus a **delivery fee** based on distance. That's it. No
subscription, no membership, no service charge from SwiftCart.

---

## Who actually delivers your order

Behind the scenes, each shop is served by a **local licensed delivery company** that
employs the riders. You don't need to think about this — you order from SwiftCart, and it
arrives.

It matters for one reason only: if there's a delivery problem, both SwiftCart support and
the delivery company's team can help, and the app routes you correctly.

---

## Your data

Your account, addresses, order history and wallet belong to **SwiftCart** — not to any
delivery company. A delivery company only sees your name, phone and address **on orders
they are actually delivering**, because their rider has to reach you. They cannot browse
SwiftCart's customers.

---

## Honest limitations

- **Coverage depends on delivery companies.** You'll see shops that a company can actually
  deliver for. Coverage grows as more companies join.
- **Delivery fees vary by area,** because different delivery companies set their own rates.
- **The app is pre-launch** — built and working, but not yet in public app stores.
