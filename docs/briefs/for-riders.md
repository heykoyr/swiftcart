# SwiftCart for Riders

*A brief for delivery riders.*

---

## The offer

**Ride for a delivery company, get paid per delivery, withdraw to your bank.**

You work for one delivery company at a time. When you're online, jobs from that company
come to your phone. Accept, collect from the shop, deliver to the customer, get paid.

---

## What you get

### Jobs pushed to your phone
Go online and you receive delivery requests from your company — with the shop, the fee and
the distance up front. Accept the ones you want.

### Turn-by-turn flow, not guesswork
The app walks you through every step: navigate to the shop → confirm arrival → collect
with a pickup code → navigate to the customer → confirm delivery with a delivery code. No
phone calls needed to work out what happens next.

### Clear, honest earnings
You see **your take-home** on every job. Your company's commission is already deducted —
what you see is what lands in your wallet. No surprises, no working out percentages.

### A wallet you control
Every completed delivery credits your wallet. Withdraw to your bank account once you're
above your company's minimum. Your bank details are encrypted; only the last four digits
are ever displayed.

### Your company's app, not a generic one
The app carries **your company's logo and colours**. It feels like your employer's app,
because it is.

### Your record
Trip history, earnings breakdown, ratings and reviews from customers, and your delivery
stats — all in one place.

---

## How you get started

1. Your company **invites your phone number**.
2. Download the SwiftCart Rider app and **sign in with that number** (one-time code).
3. Claim the invite, complete onboarding — your details, vehicle, bank account, and a
   quick liveness check.
4. Once your company approves you, go online and start receiving jobs.

---

## Moving to a different company

You can transfer — for example if another company covers your area better.

They invite your number, you open **Profile → Company Invite**, and the app tells you
plainly: *"This moves you from [current company] to [new company]."*

Two things must be done first, and the app shows them as a checklist:

1. **Withdraw your remaining balance.** Your money is owed by your *current* company and
   paid from their account — it cannot follow you to a new one.
2. **Finish any delivery in progress.** You can't leave a customer waiting mid-job.

Once both are clear, the transfer is one tap. You keep your account, your verification,
your vehicle details and your history — you do not start over.

---

## What it costs

**Nothing.** No fee to join, no subscription, no charge to withdraw beyond your company's
minimum threshold.

---

## Who pays you

**Your delivery company**, from their own payment account. SwiftCart provides the app and
the system; the company employs you and settles your earnings. If you have a question
about pay, your company's support is the right place — their contact details are in the
app.

---

## Honest limitations

- **You need an invite from a company.** You cannot sign up independently, because someone
  has to dispatch and pay you.
- **One active company at a time.** You ride for one company, and transferring is a
  deliberate process (above).
- **Your earnings depend on your company's rates.** Different companies set different
  commission and delivery rates. You always see your net.
- **If your company is suspended,** new jobs stop — but you can still withdraw what you're
  owed.
- **The platform is pre-launch.** Built and working, not yet in public app stores.
