# SwiftCart for Vendors

*A brief for shops, restaurants, pharmacies, supermarkets and other sellers.*

---

## The offer

**Sell to customers across the country, and pay SwiftCart nothing.**

List your products in the SwiftCart consumer app. Customers find you, order, and a rider
collects and delivers. You keep your full item price — the delivery fee and its commission
are a separate matter between the rider and the delivery company.

---

## What you get

### A storefront in a nationwide app
Your shop appears to every SwiftCart customer near you, sorted by distance. You do not
build an app, run a website, or pay for listing.

### Order management that fits a busy counter
Incoming orders with a push notification and a pickup code. Accept or decline. Mark ready.
The rider is dispatched automatically — you never arrange delivery yourself.

### Product and stock control
Add products with photos, prices and categories. Track stock levels, set low-stock
thresholds, and get alerts before you run out. Expiry tracking for perishables, with
warnings ahead of time.

### Promotions that bring customers back
Run discounts, "buy X get Y", bundles, free gifts and flash sales. They surface in the
customer app automatically.

### Your money
Every delivered order credits your wallet with the item subtotal. Withdraw to your bank
account whenever you're above the minimum. Your bank details are encrypted — only the
last four digits are ever shown back.

### Insight into your business
Revenue and order trends, top products, customer ratings and reviews, and analytics on
what is actually selling.

---

## What it costs

**Nothing to SwiftCart.** There is no vendor subscription and no platform commission on
your items.

You are paid the full subtotal of what you sell. Delivery economics sit entirely between
the customer, the rider and the delivery company.

> A delivery company *may* separately agree terms with you. That is between you and them —
> SwiftCart does not charge you.

---

## How you get on

You are **invited by a delivery company** operating in your area. They send an invite to
your phone number; you download the SwiftCart Vendor app, sign in with that number, and
claim it. Your shop is created automatically — no forms to chase.

Then: add your business details, upload your logo, add products, and set your opening
hours. Once approved, you are visible to customers.

---

## Who handles what

| | Who |
|---|---|
| Your products, prices, stock | **You** |
| Accepting and preparing orders | **You** |
| Collecting and delivering | The delivery company's rider |
| Taking the customer's payment | The delivery company (their payment account) |
| Paying you your subtotal | The delivery company |
| The app, the customers, the software | SwiftCart |

---

## Honest limitations

- **You need an invite.** There is no open self-signup — a delivery company covering your
  area must bring you on, because someone has to actually deliver your orders.
- **You belong to one delivery company at a time.** They are the ones who fulfil and pay
  you. (Moving between companies is possible but is a deliberate, managed process.)
- **If your company is suspended,** your shop temporarily leaves the customer app — your
  data and money are untouched, and it is reversible.
- **The platform is pre-launch.** Built and working, not yet in public app stores.
