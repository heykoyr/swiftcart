# SwiftCart for Delivery Companies

*A brief for licensed delivery businesses.*

---

## The offer

**Run a national delivery marketplace without building one.**

You bring the licence, the riders and the payment account. SwiftCart brings the consumer
app, the customers, the vendor tools, the rider app, your admin dashboard, and the entire
backend that makes it work. You pay a fixed monthly or yearly subscription — and you keep
**100% of every order's commission**.

---

## What you get

### A customer base you didn't have to acquire
Your stores appear in the SwiftCart consumer app alongside every other store in the
country. Customers already using SwiftCart can order from your vendors on day one. You
are not launching an app and begging for downloads.

### Your own apps, in your own brand
Your **rider app** and your **admin dashboard** carry your logo and your colours. Your
riders and your ops team experience it as your system, because it is. (The customer and
vendor apps stay SwiftCart-branded — that shared consumer brand is what brings you the
demand.)

### Your money, in your account
Customer payments for your orders go **directly into your own Paystack account**. SwiftCart
never holds, touches, or takes a percentage of it. Inside each payment is the vendor's
subtotal, the rider's fee, and your commission — you settle all of it from your own
balance.

### Your economics, your call
You set:
- your **commission rate** on delivery fees
- your **delivery fee per km** and minimum fee
- your **minimum withdrawal** thresholds for riders and vendors

Different companies on the platform run different numbers. Yours are yours.

### A full operations suite
Live order dispatch to your riders · live map tracking · rider and vendor onboarding and
KYC · payout processing · disputes and refunds · inventory and expiry alerts · promotions ·
customer marketing campaigns · fraud flags · audit logs.

---

## What it costs

A **flat subscription** — monthly or yearly, agreed with SwiftCart, invoiced and paid by
bank transfer. No revenue share. No per-order fee. No percentage of your commission.

Your costs do not rise with your success. If you double your order volume, your SwiftCart
bill does not move.

---

## What you need to bring

1. **An operating licence and a Paystack account.** This is the entry requirement — you
   are the licensed party handling customer money. It is the whole reason this structure
   exists.
2. **Riders.** You recruit, onboard and manage them; the app handles the rest.
3. **Vendors** (optional). You invite shops in your coverage area. They also benefit from
   nationwide customers.

---

## How onboarding works

1. SwiftCart creates your company and your admin account.
2. You sign in to the Company Admin app and set your **branding** — logo, colours, support
   contacts. Your rider app and dashboard rebrand immediately.
3. You enter your **Paystack key** (encrypted at rest; SwiftCart staff cannot read it).
   Until you do, you're marked "not live" and cannot take payments — deliberately.
4. You **invite riders and vendors** by phone number. They claim the invite after signing
   in — no extra step, no separate account.
5. You go live. Your stores appear to customers; orders dispatch to your riders.

Typical setup: **under an hour**, once your Paystack key is ready.

---

## What you can and cannot see

**You see:** your orders, your riders, your vendors, your money, your promotions, and the
name and phone of customers **on your own orders** (you run support for those deliveries —
you cannot do that blind).

**You cannot see:** SwiftCart's customer base, any other company's data, or platform-wide
figures. This is enforced in the database, not by hiding buttons — and it protects you as
much as it protects them.

---

## Honest limitations

- **You must have the payment licence.** No licence, no company. There is no workaround.
- **You do not own the customer relationship.** Customers belong to SwiftCart. If you
  leave, you leave the customers behind. That is the trade for not having to acquire them.
- **The consumer app stays SwiftCart-branded.** Customers won't see your name. Your brand
  lives with your riders and your team.
- **If your subscription lapses,** your company can be suspended: stores leave the app and
  new orders stop. Your payment account stays live so you can settle what you owe. It is
  fully reversible.
- **The platform is pre-launch.** It is built and working, but not yet in public app
  stores. Early companies are early partners.
